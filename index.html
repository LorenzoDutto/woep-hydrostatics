Structure frame vs. earth frame at the static pitch angle

Equations used (same as exercise_statics.m)

Heave equilibrium: ρw Vs(D) = Mtot gives the draft D.

CoM of the whole device (platform + ballast + turbine): zCoM = Σ mi zi / Mtot, xCoM = Σ mi xi / Mtot. Turbine CoM and hub heights are measured from the tower base. CoB = centroid of the submerged volume (only the hull contributes).

Two reference frames. The structure frame (x, z) is fixed to the platform (origin on its axis at the SWL of the upright position) and rotates with it. The earth frame (X, Z) is fixed to the ground. At a pitch angle θ: X = x cos θ + z sin θ, Z = −x sin θ + z cos θ. All the quantities above (zCoB, zCoM, J, K) are computed in the structure frame, upright, as in the MATLAB script.

CoB when the platform pitches. The CoM is fixed in the structure and just rotates with it. The CoB is not: the immersed side gains volume and the emerged side loses it, so the CoB moves towards the immersed side. For small angles it moves on an arc of radius BM = J/Vs around the metacentre M (zM = zCoB + BM, fixed in the structure): structure frame (BM sin θ, zM − BM cos θ), earth frame (zM sin θ, zM cos θ − BM), i.e. always directly below M.

Righting couple: Mtot g (XCoB − XCoM) ≈ ρw g Vs (zM − zCoM) θ − xCoM Mtot g = K θ − xCoM Mtot g

Pitch stiffness: K = Kh + Kg = ρw g (J + Vs zCoB) − Mtot g zCoM = ρw g Vs · GM, with GM = zM − zCoM

Static pitch: θ = (FT hhub + xCoM Mtot g − xCoB ρw Vs g) / K (xCoB = 0 upright, symmetric hull)

Blade tip clearance: hhub − R (upright) and −xhub sin θ + (hhub − R) cos θ (pitched), with R = 121 m; the smaller value must be ≥ the minimum clearance.

Failure checks: no room for the ballast (hb larger than the space available); sinking (Mtot > ρw Vs of the fully submerged hull); unstable (K ≤ 0); capsize (|θ| larger than the angle at which the hull top goes under water or the keel comes out of the water); blade tip clearance below the minimum.
